i.

Anatomy

The mark is the uppercase ligature Æ set in Fraunces italic, light. It sits in a 22%-radius rounded square — close to a macOS app icon mask, but with our own corner curvature.

Clear-space & misuse

Give the mark room — at least a quarter-tile of breathing space on every side. It's a literary mark, not a logo; treat it like a colophon.
guidelines

Clear-space A margin equal to a quarter of the tile width on every side. Don't crowd it; if there's no room, take it off.
